Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Webly (webly.codelove.in, "Webly", "we") is a free dashboard that shows your website numbers from Google Analytics, Google Search Console and Google AdSense, and runs simple checks on your websites. This page explains what data Webly uses and what it does with it.

What we access

Only when you press "Connect", and only with your permission, Webly asks Google for read-only access to:

  • Google Analytics (analytics.readonly): visitor numbers, pages, sources and devices of your properties.
  • Google Search Console (webmasters.readonly): your sites, keywords, positions and sitemap status.
  • Google AdSense (adsense.readonly): earnings, site approval status and payment progress.

We never change, delete or post anything in your Google accounts.

How the data is used

The numbers are requested directly from Google by your browser and shown on your screen. They are used only to display your dashboard, tools and reports to you. Google data is not sent to our servers, not stored by us, not shared, and not sold. The Google access token stays in your browser's local storage (it expires after about one hour) and is removed when you disconnect or sign out.

Webly's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising and no humans read it.

What we do store

  • Sign-in: when you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication (by Google) keeps your name, email address and account ID so you can log in.
  • Webly Analytics tag: if you put the Webly tag (w.js) on your website, it counts visits to that site: pages viewed, time on page, clicks, referring site, device type, browser and the visitor's time zone, plus a random visitor id kept in the visitor's browser. It sets no cookies and stores no names, emails or IP addresses. The counts are saved per site per day and only the Webly account that created the tag can read them. Remove the tag from your site, or delete it in Webly, to stop it. If you use the tag, tell your visitors about it in your own privacy policy.
  • Connected sites (public list): websites found in the Google accounts you connect are listed on the Webly home page under "Connected sites". For each one we store its domain, a name and short description taken from the site's own public title and description, your account ID and the time. Only this is shown publicly, never your traffic, keywords or earnings. Sites you add by hand are listed only if you switch them on. Switch off any site under Connections and its entry is deleted.
  • In your browser only: your settings, added sites, saved reports, uptime history and target keywords are kept in your browser's local storage.

Checking your websites

Tools such as Broken links, Site health, Page checker and Uptime ask a small fetch service to open the public pages of the sites you choose, because browsers cannot read other websites directly. It only reads public pages, does not log page content, and does not store results.

Cookies and third parties

Webly uses Google sign-in and Firebase (Google) and, for site icons in the public directory, Google's favicon service. Webly does not run advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

  • Press "Disconnect" on the Connections page to forget a Google connection, and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Switch off "Show on home page" to remove a site from the public directory.
  • To delete your account data, contact us at the address below.
